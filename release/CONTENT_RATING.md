## Separate owner decisions

- Choose the actual target audience. A low content rating does not mean the game is directed to children. Do not automatically select Google Play child age groups, Families participation, or Apple's Kids Category just because the character is cute.
- Confirm publisher/legal identity, ownership/licensing of all shipped material, support contact, countries, store pricing, and any local obligations. No trademark clearance or legal classification has been performed.
- If choosing child-directed distribution, review the applicable child-safety/privacy policies and required disclosures before submission. No parental gate or child-specific compliance certification is claimed.
- Confirm that screenshots and promotional copy accurately match the shipped build. There are exactly 16 existing reactions and no extra levels, achievements, sound, multiplayer, or progression system.
