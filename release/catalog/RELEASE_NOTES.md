# Don't Touch Me 0.1.1

Draft for owner review. Prepared, not published or store-approved.

The blob has opinions, and now you can hear them.

## New

- **Sound.** Kitten, puppy and robot voices, plus taps, slides, landings and boxes. Sound starts off; your choice is remembered. Settings has separate volumes for effects, voices and character sounds.
- **More ways to object.** Recoil (a startled hop) and Puffer-huff (a spiky inflation) join the reactions, for 17 regular reactions plus the wake-up.
- **Idle life.** Left alone, the blob stretches, hums, yawns, nods off mid-thought, fights sleep and twitches while dreaming.
- **Settings.** Volume, plus orientation (auto, portrait, landscape) on Android and iPhone. A privacy policy link on Android and iOS.

## Changed

- The blob falls asleep after 18 seconds of peace, down from 24.
- Annoyance builds in streaks and fades gradually once you stop.
- Jumps, slides, recoils and wake-ups always finish; poking mid-air no longer teleports the blob. Every poke still counts and makes a sound.
- Wider screens and landscape windows are supported, and short desktop windows stay usable.
- The iPhone launch screen shows the game's background instead of the engine logo.

## Notes

- **Android:** 0.1.1 uses the Google Play app ID. It cannot update the 0.1.0 Alpha test APK in place; uninstall that first.
- Windows and macOS builds are unsigned (macOS is ad-hoc signed, not notarized). Linux is not native-tested.
- Still free and offline, with no ads, accounts, purchases or analytics.
